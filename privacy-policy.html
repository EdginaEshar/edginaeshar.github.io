Overview & General Scope

Welcome to Edgina Eshar ("we", "us", or "our"). We develop and publish a multi-category portfolio of mobile applications on the Google Play Store, encompassing Utility & Productivity Tools, Wallpaper & Personalization Applications, Mobile Games, and Community Reference Catalogs. We respect your fundamental right to privacy and are committed to creating a secure, trustworthy user experience.

This Privacy Policy is universally applicable across all mobile applications published under the developer identity Edgina Eshar unless an individual application explicitly presents an alternative policy. By installing, opening, or interacting with any of our applications, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this document.

Key Principle: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Our applications are engineered to function with minimal permissions, collecting only technical telemetry strictly necessary for ad-supported free delivery, app stability, and optional user assistance.

02

Information We Collect & App Permissions

Depending on the type of application you install (Tool, Wallpaper, Game, or Catalog), we may collect the following categories of information automatically or process specific on-device permissions:

A. Automatically Collected Technical Data

When our applications launch and operate, industry-standard diagnostic and telemetry data is automatically processed through our certified third-party SDK partners:

  • Device Specifications: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version (e.g., Android 14), screen resolution, system language, and network connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) provided by Google Play Services, used strictly for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and delivering contextually relevant advertisements.
  • Application Diagnostics: App version, installation timestamps, crash logs, stack traces, and latency indicators via Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix software bugs.
  • Engagement Events: Aggregated anonymous in-app actions (e.g., screen transitions, category browsing, button clicks, session duration) via Firebase Analytics.

B. Voluntary Information Provided by You

  • Support Tickets & User Feedback: When you voluntarily submit a report, issue ticket, or suggestion via our in-app support forms, we collect the message content, chosen problem category, randomly generated ticket identifier, and local timestamp.
  • Direct Correspondence: If you contact us directly via email (e.g., for takedown requests or technical questions), we receive your email address and any attachments you include.

C. Category-Specific Permissions (Tools, Wallpapers & Games)

  • Wallpaper & Personalization Apps: To apply backgrounds to your home screen or lock screen, wallpaper apps utilize the standard Android system wallpaper manager (SET_WALLPAPER). If an app offers an optional "Save/Download" feature, it accesses your device's designated public media directory strictly to save that specific wallpaper image file. We never scan, read, access, or upload private photos or personal files from your device gallery.
  • Productivity & Utility Tools: Tool applications (e.g., calculation utilities, text processors, generators) execute computations directly on your local device hardware. Your inputs, pasted clipboard data, and calculated results are never transmitted to external servers.
  • Casual & Simulation Games: Games may store game progress, player high scores, achievement tokens, and unlocked levels exclusively on your device's private sandbox storage (SharedPreferences). No personally identifiable gameplay dossiers are created.

What We NEVER Collect: We never request, capture, or store sensitive personal information such as legal names, physical home addresses, credit/debit card numbers, banking credentials, phone contacts, microphone audio, camera feeds, or precise GPS coordinates.

03

How Collected Information Is Used

We process the aforementioned technical data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Core Service Functionality: To deliver in-app features, populate catalog content, execute clipboard utilities, and store non-critical user preferences locally.
  • Crash Diagnostic & Performance Optimization: Identifying software anomalies, rendering errors, and hardware incompatibilities to ensure smooth app performance across thousands of Android device models.
  • Advertising Delivery & Monetization: Monetizing free applications through Google AdMob and certified ad mediation networks (including Meta Audience Network, Liftoff Monetize / Vungle, AppLovin, and Unity Ads), serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads adhering strictly to Google Play Families and Advertising policies.
  • Customer Support & Issue Resolution: Investigating reports regarding broken catalog items, ad display glitches, or user-submitted questions.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Enforcing rate-limits (e.g., cooldown periods on feedback tickets) and safeguarding against automated bot activity or fraudulent ad impressions.

05

Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We practice data minimization and do not maintain permanent user profiles:

  • In-App Support Tickets: Stored on our secure Firebase database with an automated First-In-First-Out (FIFO) retention ceiling (limited to a maximum of 50 total records). Older entries are automatically and permanently purged as new ones arrive.
  • Device Preferences: Data such as unlocked utility states, language selections, and local timestamps remain exclusively on your device's private storage (SharedPreferences) and are completely erased whenever you clear app data or uninstall the application.
  • Analytics & Crash Telemetry: Retained by Google Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics under their standard retention schedules (typically 2 to 14 months) in anonymized aggregate formats.

How to Request Data Deletion: If you have submitted a feedback ticket or communication and wish to have all associated records immediately purged, simply send an email to eka.indriyani013@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and your ticket number. We will fulfill your request within 48 business hours.

Children's Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children's online privacy is of the utmost importance. Our applications are intended for a general audience or young gaming enthusiasts, and we strictly abide by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K (General Data Protection Regulation for Kids), and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • AdMob configurations in our applications adhere strictly to non-personalized ad standards or child-directed flags wherever applicable under Google Play target audience classifications.
  • If we become aware that personal information of a child under 13 has inadvertently been collected without verified parental consent, we will take immediate measures to delete such data from our servers.

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we extend full respect to your statutory privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area (GDPR) / UK: You have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or request erasure of your data, as well as the right to withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via your device's Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID).
  • California Residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal categories are collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We confirm that we do not sell your personal data to third parties for monetary consideration.
08

Data Security Measures

We deploy robust administrative and technological security safeguards to protect all transmitted data:

  • All network communication between our applications and servers (such as Firebase Realtime Database or API endpoints) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Access to backend databases is strictly restricted by declarative Firebase Security Rules that prevent unauthorized public read/write access.
